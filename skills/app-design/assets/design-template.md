# The look

> Written in the person's language. The values are copied into the app's
> tokens file when building. Delete this note.

**Direction:** _Clean utility_: _neutral greys, a crisp sans, plain direct language_
**Why:** _Chị Mai's regulars should feel it's quick and no-nonsense._
**Chosen:** _2026-10-27_

## Colours
| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Page background (ground) | `#f4f5f7` |
| Cards and inputs (surface) | `#ffffff` |
| Main text (ink) | `#1d2433` |
| Secondary text (ink-soft) | `#5a6274` |
| Borders (line) | `#d9dee7` |
| Buttons and highlights (accent) | `#2f5fd0` |
| Text on buttons (accent-ink) | `#ffffff` |
| Success / warning / error | `#2e7d4f` / `#a0660a` / `#c0392b` |

## Type
- Headings: _Public Sans, 600_
- Body: _Public Sans, 15px_
- Scale: _regular_

## Shape
- Corners: _8px_ · Borders: _1px_ · Spacing: _regular_ · Shadows: _soft_

## Voice
- _Write plainly. Say what a thing does; don't sell it._
- _Short sentences, one idea each._
- _Buttons say the verb and its object: "Save booking", not "Submit"._
- _Empty pages say what the space is for and what to do first._
- _Error messages say what happened and what to do next, never a bare code._

## Never
- _Purple-blue gradients, glass effects, emoji as icons, lorem ipsum._
