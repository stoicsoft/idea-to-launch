# Questions and answers

**Do I need to know how to code?**
No. You describe what you want and answer a few questions. The assistant
explains any technical word the first time it has to use one. If you ever
don't understand something, say "I don't understand" and it will explain
again more simply.

**Which AI tool should I use?**
To build a real app, use one that can work with files on your computer:
the Code tab in Claude Desktop, Claude Code, the Codex app, Cursor, Gemini
CLI or GitHub Copilot. If you only use a chat app, you can still shape the
idea, plan it, choose a look and get a clickable prototype.

**Does it cost anything?**
The skills are free (MIT license). Your AI tool's usual plan applies.
Putting an app online usually costs a few dollars a month for hosting, and
about $10–15 a year for a `.com` address. The assistant tells you before
anything costs money, and you create those accounts yourself.

**Can it build any app?**
It's best at web apps that people use on their phones or computers:
booking pages, order forms, sign-ups, internal tools, simple shops,
dashboards and landing pages. For an app in the App Store or Google Play,
it recommends starting with a phone-friendly web app and explains what a
store app would involve.

**Which language can I use?**
Any. The assistant answers in the language you write in. Your app can be in
your customers' language, or several languages at once. It's tested in 20
languages.

**Where is my project saved?**
In your project folder: the app's code, plus a `product/` folder with
simple pages (your idea, plan, look, decisions and check results). In chat
apps, the assistant gives you these pages to keep, and you paste them back
to continue.

**Can I switch between Claude, Codex and Cursor?**
Yes. Open the same folder in the other tool and say "Let's continue". It
reads `product/progress.md` and picks up where you left off.

**Will it put something online or charge my card without asking?**
No. Going online, buying anything, emails to real customers, payments,
deleting data and changing who can see what always wait for your explicit
"yes" to that exact step. Your AI tool's own permission prompts still apply
on top of this.

**Is my customers' data safe?**
The skills build apps that keep each person's information private by
default, never delete saved information when changing the app, and keep
secret keys out of the code. A check (app-review) looks for these
problems before launch. For health, payment or other regulated data, it
will tell you when you need a professional review.

**What if something breaks?**
Say what you saw (a screenshot helps). The fix step finds the real cause,
fixes only that, proves it works, and tells you in one sentence what was
wrong.

**Can a developer take over later?**
Yes. The code is standard, readable and in English, with tests and a README.
The `product/` pages explain what was built and why.

**How do I update or remove it?**
Run the install command again to update. Use `uninstall` to remove it (see
[install](install.md#update-check-remove)). Your projects are never touched.

**Does it send my data anywhere?**
The skills themselves are plain text. They don't run in the background,
collect data or call any server. Your AI tool works as it normally does.
